# Fitness Achieve video editor preferences
Before every edit ask:
1. Where will the video be posted?
2. What action should viewers take?
3. What editing style is desired?

Default when specified: 9:16 Instagram/Facebook Reels, English on-screen text, premium clean dynamic editing, readable captions, appropriate motion graphics, music and sound effects. Use only authentic supplied footage and approved brand assets. Do not fabricate people, poses or exercises. Avoid unnecessary setup clips such as camera adjustment or clothing adjustment. Preserve movement highlights. Always present a preview for approval before final export. Brand rules are job-specific; do not apply Fitness Achieve branding to other client projects.
